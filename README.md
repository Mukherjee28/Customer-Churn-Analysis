## 📌 Project Overview
Customer retention is one of the most critical business challenges for subscription-based companies. Acquiring a new customer often costs significantly more than retaining an existing one.
This project analyzes customer churn data to identify the factors influencing customer attrition and uncover actionable insights that can help businesses improve customer retention strategies.
The project combines **SQL for data extraction**, **Python for data cleaning & exploratory analysis**, and **Power BI for interactive business dashboards**, simulating a real-world analytics workflow.

# 🎯 Business Problem
The company has observed an increasing number of customers discontinuing their services.
The objectives of this analysis are to:
- Understand why customers leave
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Measure the impact of contract types and payment methods
- Discover demographic patterns
- Build an executive dashboard for business stakeholders
- Recommend strategies to reduce churn

# 📌 Key Business Insights
Some major insights obtained during the analysis include:
- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** showed the highest churn.
- Higher monthly charges were associated with increased churn risk.
- Customers with shorter tenure were more likely to leave.
- Electronic check users had comparatively higher churn.
- Long-term contracts significantly improved customer retention.

# 💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis:
### ✅ Encourage Long-Term Contracts
Provide discounts for annual or two-year subscriptions.
### ✅ Focus on New Customers
Implement onboarding programs during the first few months.
### ✅ Improve Customer Experience
Enhance support for customers showing early signs of dissatisfaction.
### ✅ Personalized Retention Campaigns
Target high-risk customers with customized offers before they churn.
